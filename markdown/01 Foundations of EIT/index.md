What EIT measures and how it is modelled: the physics that leads to the conductivity equation, and the models of the electrodes through which current enters and voltages are read.

**Reading order.**

1. [[Electrical Impedance Tomography]], with its place among [[Tomographic Imaging Modalities]] and its [[Applications of EIT]].
2. The physics: [[Ohm's Law in Continuum Form]], [[Continuity Equation]] and the [[Quasi-Static Approximation]] lead to the [[Conductivity Equation]]; [[Complex Conductivity]] covers alternating currents.
3. The electrodes: [[Electrode Models]] compares the continuum, [[Point Electrode Model|point]], [[Gap Model|gap]], [[Shunt Model|shunt]] and [[Complete Electrode Model|complete electrode]] models.
4. The experiment: [[Measurement Protocols]] and [[Current Patterns]].
